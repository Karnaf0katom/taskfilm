# Changelog

## 0.1.0a2

- Preview navigation and filters for product films, feature lessons, motion graphics, OSS walkthroughs and recordings.
- A gallery can retain up to eight hash-verified movies; browser checks use each movie's retained duration and dimensions.
- A separate 48-second Excalidraw motion-promo recipe with six narration cues, licensed music, perspective, camera moves and animation of the saved native diagram.

## 0.1.0a1

First public alpha, published as Taskfilm under the MIT License:

- Local CLI and installable agent skill for feature lessons, product promos and OSS walkthroughs.
- Original browser Taskboard sample with captured actions and retained result evidence.
- Editable 31-second product-film kit, including camera moves, click effects, recap and synthesized sound.
- Separate 24-second HTML/SVG animation study.
- Real OSS capture recipes for Excalidraw diagrams, a narrated drawDB schema lesson and Penpot design handoff.
- Pinned repository source packs and an evidence-backed HTML companion.
- Portable wheel and source distribution with retained asset and third-party notices.
- A checksum-verified static preview deployment workflow for GitHub Pages.
- HyperFrames 0.8.134 and a complete-render audio regression in CI.

Linux is the checked platform. Other operating systems remain unverified.
A fresh normal 31-second render preserves the original mix within
0.06 LU and fully decodes; it needs no separate audio assembly. The previously
accepted product film remains preserved with its original production evidence.
