# Motion lab design

Concept: one editable timeline turns type, a path and a flat layout into three
different kinds of movement.

Palette: deep ink #11191c, warm paper #f5efe5, coral #ff6947, supporting ink
#53645b and dark-stage muted text #a5b7b5. Type: bundled Manrope for large
display/body text; bundled Geist Mono for edge labels and technical detail.

The type scene anchors its large three-line lockup to the left and a moving
orbital marker to the right. The vector scene uses a light field and a split
layout: language left, shape right. The depth scene travels through three
cards and resolves to a readable flat composition. The close changes to a
full coral field and a large wordmark. Edge labels carry chapter orientation.

Motion changes by scene: masked rise / side snap / rotation; continuous SVG
draw and shape interpolation; staggered 3D assembly and camera movement;
letter-by-letter perspective lockup. Hard cuts separate the visual studies.
Each scene holds its resolved state long enough to read. Every scene has its
own paused, seekable timeline and is mounted as a sub-composition.
